## Who we are

Redlimit is an Indonesian cybersecurity company founded in 2022. Our work splits three ways:

- **Services** — penetration testing, network and infrastructure security audits, managed security monitoring, security training, and custom web and mobile application development.
- **Products** — security platforms we build and run ourselves, listed below.
- **Community** — [Meta4sec](#meta4sec), the ethical hacking community we started and still run.

Our consultants are certified practitioners, and the people who write our tooling are the same people who run the engagements. That is the whole idea: everything here is built by operators, for operators.

## Learning paths

The CTF Lab is organised around three tracks, each ending in a Redlimit certification:

**Red Team / Bug Bounty** — recon and attack surface mapping, the vulnerability classes bug bounty programs actually pay for (XSS, SQLi, SSRF, IDOR, auth bypass) with filter-bypass technique, advanced classes (race conditions, deserialization, SSTI, GraphQL, business logic), and writing reports a triager accepts. Tools: Burp Suite, ffuf, Nmap, sqlmap.

**CTF Player** — full jeopardy coverage: web exploitation, cryptography from classical ciphers through RSA and padding oracle attacks, pwn and reverse engineering, forensics and OSINT. Tools: Ghidra, pwntools, CyberChef, Wireshark.

**SOC Analyst** — log analysis and SIEM, incident response following the NIST cycle, digital forensics across disk, memory, network and email, and threat hunting mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Tools: Splunk, Wazuh, Volatility, Zeek.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Found a security issue in one of our platforms? We want to know, and we will treat you well for telling us.

Report it to [sales@redlimit.id](mailto:sales@redlimit.id) with the affected asset, reproduction steps, and impact. Please give us a reasonable window to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly, and keep testing non-destructive — no denial of service, no social engineering, and no access to data that is not yours.
